## Notes for the table

- Self-selection is trust-based by design. Nothing stops someone picking another seat; this is
  the Foundry model and it is the right one for a home game.
- A reconnect drops mic capture — players must re-click the mic after any reload.
- Own-cursor is suppressed client-side: a player will NOT see their own hover dot, only other
  people's. Clicks they do see. Say this out loud or someone reports it as a bug.
- Cursor colour is derived from the NAME, so each seat gets a stable distinct tint for free.
