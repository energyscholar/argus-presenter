## `home`
**Arabus**

Sub-brown dwarf. Hex 1235. No star lights it — it glows from its own heat, a billion years of slow contraction.

Typhon orbits close, venting sulfur. Where the moon's small field meets the giant's unconfined one, the interaction zone burns.

The quiet hemisphere is skimmable. The other side is not.
